Put the competition logos here (transparent PNG works best, ~600 px wide):
  league.png     -> ?league=hvl       (Volley League)
  leaguecup.png  -> ?league=cup       (League Cup)
  supercup.png   -> ?league=supercup  (Super Cup)
The stinger shows the one matching its ?league= while the screen is covered. ?logo=URL overrides, ?logo=0 hides it.
